About SMTE

SMTE is organised by the STEM Research Group in Curtin University’s School of Education, formerly the Science and Mathematics Education Centre (SMEC), often co-hosted with international partners. It brings together researchers, teacher educators, teachers and students to share research and practice in STEM education. Recent conferences were held with Punjabi University, India (2024), and Jiaxing University, China (2025). The 13th conference comes to Papua New Guinea in 2027.
