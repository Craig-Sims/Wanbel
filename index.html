Wanbel · one mind

A joint initiative of Pacific Adventist University and Curtin University, supported by Australia Awards Papua New Guinea, bringing academics, researchers and educational leaders from across PNG and the Pacific together to build collaborative research.

The thread through every session was people. Relationships between institutions and colleagues are what move research and collaboration forward.

Aims

What the symposium set out to do

  1. 01Connect researchers and educators across PNG and the Pacific.
  2. 02Generate research questions grounded in our own teaching, leadership and practice.
  3. 03Form research clusters across institutions and disciplines.
  4. 04Build towards presenting this research at SMTE 2027.
